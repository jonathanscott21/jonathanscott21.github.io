Personal Archive Sync

A personal, single-user utility that copies a local Markdown knowledge archive and its supporting files to its owner's Google Drive and verifies those copies.

The utility runs on the owner's Mac using rclone. Its configured destination is one designated Drive folder. This public site supplies application information and a privacy disclosure; it does not provide access to the archive or a web-based synchronization service.

Google authorization is used to list and read managed Drive files, upload new files, and update known files after integrity and conflict checks. It is not an authorization service for other users.
